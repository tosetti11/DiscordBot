Place your DraftKings golf round score over/under screenshot images here.

Supported formats: .jpg, .jpeg, .png, .webp

Then run:
  node scripts/post-golf-round-totals.js --tournament "Tournament Name" --round "Round 1"

Or preview first:
  node scripts/post-golf-round-totals.js --dry-run --tournament "Tournament Name" --round "Round 1"
